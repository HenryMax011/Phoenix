# PhoenixBor Design System (Master)

## Direction
Elite industrial luxury — Prada-like restraint for vedação industrial.
Black + strong blue. No yellow. Huge typography, sparse chrome, intentional motion.

## Motion
- Hero: staggered fade/slide (0.8–1s, ease [0.22, 1, 0.36, 1])
- About carousel: Ken Burns image + text slide (AnimatePresence)
- Auto-advance 7s; manual arrows
- Scroll reveal via Framer Motion `useInView`
- Hover: letter-spacing expand on CTAs, soft scale on icons
